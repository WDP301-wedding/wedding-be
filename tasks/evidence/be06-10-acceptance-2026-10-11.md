## Additional executable acceptance proof

`tests/integration/commercial-acceptance-api-cases.ts` adds five cases to the actual
HTTP/Identity/owner-composition/PostgreSQL suite:

1. Catalog change rejects stale draft submission; explicit re-selection produces
   total 47,000,003 VND and deposit 14,100,001 VND. Later price/archive edits cannot
   change approved document/PDF/eligibility. Database guards deny contract amount,
   line text, approval deletion and PDF-byte tampering (even with a matching new hash).
2. Full-contract checkout versus contract withdrawal: one success, one 409, never
   both; rejected/withdrawn booking has no attempt or charge.
3. Full-contract checkout versus timeline successor: one success, one 409, with
   matching timeline/attempt state and no premature receivable.
4. UNKNOWN plus an expired hold denies the same hall/date/shift to another booking.
   Verified signed failure releases the pin; withdrawal and competing hold succeed,
   without a ledger payment or Couple promotion.
5. A trigger fails PAYMENT insertion after CONTRACT registration. Both monetary
   writes and their outbox roll back, while accepted SUCCEEDED provider proof stays
   durable. Status reconciliation then confirms/promotes once; IPN replay leaves
   exactly one CHARGE, one PAYMENT and one application.

The harness checks that both racing transactions really wait on PostgreSQL locks
before releasing them. It holds both the Booking advisory lock and Booking row:
timeline and payment owners use different serialization mechanisms. Date fixtures
roll into the next month instead of generating invalid February dates.
Fault triggers and artificial hold aging occur only in newly created disposable
loopback databases; no shared data or provider state is modified by these tests.
Controlled VNPay evidence is offline, separate from genuine sandbox proof.
