## Verified behavior

The four new real HTTP/PostgreSQL acceptance cases pass individually through
`scripts/staff-operations.verify.mjs` using disposable databases on loopback
PostgreSQL. All migrations, including the new additive
`20261104100000_appendix_print_requests`, apply successfully there.

1. Two saved designs, 100 + 40 copies, produce a server preview of 700,000 VND.
   Couple consent retains the exact previews and appendix approval with no order
   or charge. Manager's same-version appendix approval creates one order with two
   lines and one appendix ledger charge. Idempotent replay creates neither twice.
2. A successor appendix changes one quantity to 125 and removes the other design.
   Pending changes block production while retaining the effective order. Mutual
   approval synchronizes it to 625,000 VND. Production then succeeds and later
   quantity changes are rejected.
3. Wrong digest and unauthorized ownership fail; withdrawing a pending request
   creates no order. Unauthenticated/unauthorized production is denied.
4. Concurrent confirmations retain one request; concurrent second approvals
   produce one effective approval. Full removal cancels the order and records
   one 700,000 VND credit. Direct SQL cannot revive the cancelled order.
